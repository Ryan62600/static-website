Destinations

Seven main islands' worth of coastline, jungle, and reef — here's where our tours actually go.

Palawan

El Nido & Coron

Island-hopping through hidden lagoons, shipwreck diving, and limestone cliffs rising straight out of turquoise water.

Surigao del Norte

Siargao

Surf lessons at Cloud 9, island hopping to Naked Island and Guyam, and rock pool hopping at Magpupungko.

Bohol

Bohol

The Chocolate Hills, tarsier sanctuaries, and a floating lunch cruise down the Loboc River.

Aklan

Boracay

White Beach sunsets, island bar-hopping, and watersports on some of the finest sand in Southeast Asia.

Ifugao

Banaue Rice Terraces

2,000-year-old rice terraces carved into the mountains by the Ifugao — often called the "Stairway to Heaven."

Cebu

Cebu & Oslob

Swim alongside whale sharks, canyoneer through Kawasan Falls, and explore the historic streets of Cebu City.
