About Isla Tours PH

Founded by a family of Palawan boatmen, built into a nationwide island-touring company.

Our story

Isla Tours PH started in 2012 with a single outrigger boat in Puerto Princesa, ferrying backpackers to lagoons most maps hadn't caught up with yet. Word spread, the boat became three, and three became a network of local guides across the Visayas, Luzon, and Mindanao.

Today we run fixed-departure and private tours to over 30 destinations — but every itinerary is still built the way it was in 2012: by people who actually live there.

What we stand for

Local-first guiding

Every tour is led by a guide from that province — not a call center itinerary. They know which cove is quiet at 7am and which one isn't.

Fair pay for boat crews

Boatmen and guides are paid a fixed day rate, not a commission cut, so the trip doesn't turn into a string of souvenir stops.

Reef-safe practices

No anchoring on coral, no touching marine life, and a hard cap on group size at snorkel and dive sites.
